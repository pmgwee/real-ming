Real-Ming

A private personal operations assistant, used by one person.

Real-Ming coordinates its operator's own commitments across personal, business, academic and content responsibilities. It reads the operator's calendar and task records, reconciles them, and produces a daily brief and end-of-day summary.

Who can use it

Nobody else. Real-Ming is a single-operator system with one authorised identity. It is not a product, has no sign-up, and accepts no other users.

What it connects to

  • Google Calendar — reads events to reconcile scheduled commitments, and creates or updates events the operator asks it to.
  • Notion — reads and updates the operator's own task records.
  • Telegram — a private bot that only accepts commands from the operator.

What it will not do

  • Send an external message without explicit, per-action approval.
  • Move money, make a payment, or place a trade. There is no such capability.
  • Delete a calendar. It is not granted that permission.
  • Share, sell, or transfer data to any third party.
Consequential actions stop and wait for a human decision bound to one exact target. An approval that is granted becomes invalid the moment its target changes.
