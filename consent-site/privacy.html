Privacy Policy

Real-Ming · last updated 28 August 2026

Real-Ming is a private personal operations assistant operated by one individual for their own use. It has a single authorised user, no sign-up, and no other users. This policy describes how that operator's own data is handled.

Data accessed

  • Google Calendar — event details on calendars the operator has access to, using the calendar.events and calendar.calendarlist.readonly scopes. Events are read to reconcile commitments, and created or updated when the operator requests it.
  • Notion — the operator's own task records.
  • Telegram — commands sent by the operator to a private bot.

How it is used

Solely to produce the operator's own daily brief and end-of-day summary, to reconcile scheduled commitments against task records, and to carry out work the operator explicitly requests. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or model training.

Google user data

Real-Ming's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google Calendar data is used only to provide the operator-facing features described above. It is not transferred to any third party, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train any generalised model. No human other than the operator reads it.

Storage and retention

Data is held in infrastructure controlled by the operator. Derived knowledge is stored encrypted at rest. Raw ingested material is deleted on a defined retention schedule once it has been verified; audit records of what happened are kept without retaining the underlying sensitive payload.

Sharing

None. Data is not shared, sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. There are no advertising, analytics, or data-broker integrations.

Credentials

Authentication material is stored outside source control and is never placed into logs, task records, review output, or model prompts. It can be revoked or rotated at any time without changing the application.

Revoking access

Google access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions. Revocation immediately and permanently ends Real-Ming's access to Google Calendar data.
