Architecture verdict

The earlier sketch remains the spine—not the final map.

Its direction was right: authoritative sources, scoped connectors, a Real-Ming control plane, Hermes, Agent Brain, durable workflows, Telegram, and workers. The v1.1 design adds the governed, persistent Knowledge Vault without turning it into a universal memory.

Diagram E · Human authority model

Automation expands by risk tier, never by title.

Every decision evaluates the actor, Executive Role, Work Item, Trust Domain, adapter capability, action, reversibility, risk, Standing Authority, and exact target artifact.

Tier 1 · automatic

Observe & draft

  • Read, monitor, classify, summarize, reconcile, and recommend.
  • Draft messages, plans, code, content, and record changes without sending or promoting.
  • Produce cited evidence and Outcome Reports.
Tier 2 · standing authority

Low-risk reversible action

  • Only within an explicit, narrow, revocable policy grant.
  • Target, capability, limits, expiry, and rollback remain recorded.
  • All effects are verified and auditable.
Tier 3 · CEO approval

Exact high-risk artifact

  • External communication, production promotion, destructive or permission action.
  • Purchase or financial Record Change outside approved scope.
  • Changed target invalidates Approval; scopes cannot be silently bundled.
×

Hard exclusions in v1

  • No Money Movement, payment initiation, bank transfer, brokerage trading, or self-approved financial transaction.
  • No academic submission on Ming's behalf.
  • No arbitrary direct push to a production branch, direct production deploy, or “deploy latest” control.
  • No indiscriminate ingestion of the full Notion workspace, email history, local filesystem, or every Agent Brain project.

Locked architecture decisions

  • CEO-led single-user-first system with future workspace and actor boundaries.
  • Five direct-report Executive Roles; COO coordinates without peer authority.
  • Telegram primary, dashboard for monitoring, exact Approval, and recovery.
  • Master Tasks canonical; Sources of Record remain authoritative.
  • Brokered context, Compiled Knowledge, and Project Evidence; hybrid workers; tiered authority; and no Money Movement.

Deferred operational inputs

  • Exact first DuitSini product update.
  • Selected Personal Context files and allowlisted Notion page identifiers.
  • OAuth credentials, provider accounts, connector secrets, and scheduler inventory.
  • Provider/project budgets beneath the RM250 global cap.
  • Staged Agent Brain registration and verification of additional projects.
  • Durable encrypted volume, backup, and optional synchronization provider for the Knowledge Vault.
