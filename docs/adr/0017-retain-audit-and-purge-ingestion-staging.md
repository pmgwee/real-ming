# Retain audit and purge ingestion staging

Verified raw ingestion payloads and staging files are deleted after 30 days unless a stricter Trust Domain policy applies; their Candidate Envelope identifiers, hashes, tombstones, and purge evidence remain append-only without retaining the sensitive payload. Active Personal Context and Compiled Knowledge projections remain while current, superseded personal projections are retained for 12 months, and other compiled generations follow their Trust Domain policy. Financial Snapshot versions, CEO Approvals, Outcome Reports, and audit events are retained indefinitely for v1; Sensitive Secrets never enter tasks, prompts, compiled knowledge, outcomes, or audit logs, and deleted Context Vault or Knowledge Vault content must age out of backups within 30 days.
